# matchfoundr Co-Pilot — 15s launch film · beat map

130 BPM · 1 beat = 0.4615 s = 27.69 frames @ 60 fps · 32 beats = 14.77 s = 886 frames · 1920×1080 · one take.
Song starts 12 beats before its drop, so the song's drop lands on beat 12 (5.54 s, frame 332).

## Story

A designer tells the Co-Pilot they're tired of building alone. The Co-Pilot dot thinks, the drop reveals six
co-founder matches, it lands on Anna (94 fit), offers a Friday call, the intro is sent, and the two paths
converge into the matchfoundr mark.
