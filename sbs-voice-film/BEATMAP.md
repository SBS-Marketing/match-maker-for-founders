# SBS Voice-Agent — beat map

**Format:** 36.6 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps, one continuous take, German. 100 BPM, 1 beat = 0.6 s.

**Timing:** Every time below is derived from the measured voice lines in `timing.js`; the film re-times itself if the audio changes. The music's bar 1 starts at the pickup (2.40 s).

## Story

1. A customer calls Elektro Wagner's emergency line. The phone rings, the call shows up in the island, and the SBS voice agent takes the call.
2. The conversation is transcribed live, word by word.
3. Each key word the customer or the agent says lifts out of the transcript as a chip. It flies into the dark „Im Hintergrund erledigt“ card and becomes a line of work:
   - the request and its priority
   - the CRM contact
   - the calendar slot
   - the SMS
4. After the call ends, the island confirms that the SMS went out.
5. The card floods the frame. Its four checks merge and become the SBS helmet.
6. The agent says the claim: „Ihr Telefon ist ab heute nie mehr besetzt.“

## Palette and type

Taken from the SBS website:

- **Canvas:** `#f6f8fc` with a 56 px grid.
- **Ink:** `#0b1220`.
- **Emerald:** `#10b981`, `#059669` and `#047857`.
- **Mint:** `#6ee7b7` and `#e6fbf3`.
- **Check:** `#22c55e`.
- **Peach (priority only):** `#fdd9bf`.
- **Black (end card):** `#050505`.
- **Red:** `#dc2626`, used only on the decline button.
- **Type:** Sora 500/600 for headlines and Manrope 500–700 for the UI.

## Beats

### 0.00–2.40 · Es klingelt

- **Picture:**
  - Lock screen at 07:52.
  - The island springs into the call banner: „Notdienst-Leitung · Eingehender Anruf“ with decline and accept buttons.
  - On beats 1 and 2 the phone buzzes and rings leave the accept button.
  - At 1.50 the title rolls to „KI-Agent nimmt an …“ and the SBS helmet rises into the accept button.
- **Camera:** Tight on the island (2.4×), slow pan down.
- **Sound:**
  - Island bloop.
  - Marimba ring motif E–G#–B–E with a buzz.
  - Glass tick on the takeover.

### 2.40–3.60 · KI nimmt an

- **Picture:**
  - The accept button flies into the call screen and becomes the website's dark helmet orb, with its pulse rings.
  - The banner shrinks into the live island (mini bars and timer).
  - The call screen rises: „SBS Voice-Agent · Unbekannte Nummer · ● Live“, 32 voice bars, the status line and „Live-Transkript“.
- **Camera:** Pull back from 2.4× to 1.12× onto the whole phone.
- **Sound:**
  - Pickup chime; the music enters on bar 1.
  - Orb whoosh and land thump.

### 3.60–10.20 · Das Anliegen

- **Picture:**
  - L1 and L2 are transcribed word by word, in emerald KI-Agent bubbles and white Anruferin bubbles.
  - The bars switch colour with the speaker: emerald for the agent, navy for the caller.
  - Spoken key words get a marker.
- **Camera:** Slow drift right.
- **Sound:** Pad, sub and a soft kick on the one, ducked under the voices.

### 10.20–11.70 · Hintergrund startet

- **Picture:**
  - The chip „Sicherung raus“ peels off the caller's bubble.
  - The navy card grows out of the landed chip.
  - The row reads „Anliegen erkannt: Sicherung löst aus“.
  - The empty slots for CRM, Kalender and SMS rise.
  - Check 1 at 11.40.
- **Camera:** Reveal pan to the card, slightly out.
- **Sound:** Whoosh, card thump, check tick.

### 11.70–18.00 · Priorität und Kundendaten

- **Picture:**
  - „dringend“ flies in and floods into the peach tag „Priorität hoch“ (12.60).
  - „Sabine Krüger“ and „Hafenstraße 12“ fly into the CRM row: „Kundin im CRM angelegt“.
  - Check 2 at 18.00.
- **Camera:** Slow push.

### 18.00–23.44 · Terminvorschlag

- **Picture:**
  - „Frau Krüger“ swaps the call header from „Unbekannte Nummer“ to her name.
  - „Morgen um 8 Uhr“ flies into row 3: „Termin morgen 08:00 im Kalender“ with the status „wartet auf Zusage …“.
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Sound:** Bar 9 thins to pad and sub, for suspense under „Passt das?“.

### 23.44–31.20 · Zusage und Bestätigung

- **Picture:**
  - A check lifts off the caller's „Ja,“ (23.70), grows on its way and floods row 3 (24.30): „… eingetragen“.
  - „gebucht!“ becomes the website's „Termin gebucht“ pill (27.30).
  - „Bestätigung“ becomes row 4: „SMS-Bestätigung versendet · Techniker informiert“ (29.40).
  - The call ends: „Gespräch beendet · alles erledigt“, „Beendet“.
  - The call header clears, and the island opens to „✓ SMS gesendet · Termin morgen 08:00 · an Sabine Krüger“ (29.70).
- **Camera:** Slow push.
- **Sound:**
  - Booking chime.
  - Pill pop.
  - Hang-up blip.
  - The ring motif returns as the SMS sound.

### 31.20–34.80 · Alles erledigt, then the claim

- **Picture:**
  - On the bar-13 downbeat the navy card floods the frame.
  - Each row's text falls out through its mask as its check leaves; the four checks merge into the website's success circle (31.90).
  - Black floods out of it (32.10).
  - The claim rises word by word with the voice-over.
- **Camera:** Push into the flood, 1.12× to 1.45×.
- **Sound:** Flood swell, merge, dark whoosh, voice-over.

### 34.80–36.60 · SBS

- **Picture:**
  - On the spoken „besetzt.“ the empty circle shrinks behind the growing SBS helmet. The helmet slides left and the wordmark wipes on from its edge, S-B-S.
  - „besetzt.“ gets its emerald underline.
  - The line „— Der Voice-Agent von SBS“ rises. The finished end card reads for about a second while the camera keeps pushing.
- **Camera:** End push.
- **Sound:** Logo hit with the E add9 chord.

## Rules check

- **Camera:** It only pulls back until 11.70 and only pushes in afterwards. Its x and y never decrease, so no move reverses.
- **Pace:** No hold is longer than 1 s; the bars, the orb and the word rises keep moving through the dialogue.
- **No fades:** Colour changes are clip floods. Rings thin their stroke. Text rises from mask lines.
